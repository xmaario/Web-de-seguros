Manage your choices

You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time using the button below or the "Privacy settings" link in the footer. Analytics cookies are only set after you accept. Rejecting cookies does not stop you from using the website.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Guides for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website or its partners store on your device. They can remember your preferences, help measure how a site is used, or allow advertising to be shown and measured.

Cookies we use

Our own storage: UnpackInsurance does not set its own cookies. When you choose Accept or Reject in our cookie banner, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (key ui_consent) so we do not ask again. This is strictly necessary to respect your choice. The estimate calculator works without storing anything on your device. We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across other websites.
