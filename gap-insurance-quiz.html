Do I need gap insurance? Take the quiz

Gap insurance pays the difference between what you owe on a car loan or lease and what the car is worth if it is totaled or stolen. These six questions show whether that gap is likely in your case. It takes about a minute and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

1. How did you pay for the car?
2. Did you put down less than 20%?
3. Is the loan or lease 60 months or longer?
4. Did you roll a balance from a previous car into this loan?
5. Did you buy it new within the last two years?
6. Do you drive more than 15,000 miles a year?

How the quiz works

A gap appears when your loan balance falls more slowly than your car loses value. The quiz counts the situations that make this more likely:

  • A small down payment. With less than about 20% down, you start the loan owing close to, or more than, the car's value.
  • A long loan term. Loans of 60 months or more pay down the balance slowly in the early years.
  • Rolled-over negative equity. Adding an old loan balance to a new loan creates a gap from day one, so it counts double.
  • A new car. New cars usually lose value fastest in the first years.
  • High mileage. Driving a lot can lower a car's value faster than average.

If you enter what you owe and what the car is worth, the quiz also shows the gap today. Learn more in What Is Gap Insurance? and What It Means to Be Upside Down on a Car Loan.

What to do with your result

  1. Check your contract. Many leases already include a gap waiver, and some loans include gap coverage you may already be paying for.
  2. Compare where you can buy it. Gap coverage may be offered by your auto insurer, your lender, or the dealership, and the price and terms can differ.
  3. Review it over time. As you pay down the loan, the gap shrinks. Once you owe less than the car is worth, you may be able to cancel gap coverage and ask about a refund of any unused portion.

Common questions

Is gap insurance required?

No state requires it by law, but a lender or leasing company may require it in the contract. Check your paperwork or ask the lender.

Does gap insurance cover my deductible?

Some gap policies cover the collision or comprehensive deductible and some do not. Read the terms before you buy.

Do I need gap insurance if I paid cash?

No. Without a loan or lease, there is no balance for gap coverage to pay off.
