What does each car coverage pay for?

  • Liability pays for injuries and damage you cause to other people and their property, like the other car and the pedestrian. Required in nearly every state.
  • Collision repairs your own car after a crash with another vehicle or an object, no matter who caused it.
  • Comprehensive covers your car for non-crash events such as hail, theft, fire, flooding, vandalism, and hitting an animal.
  • Uninsured motorist coverage protects you when the at-fault driver has no insurance or not enough to pay for your injuries.
