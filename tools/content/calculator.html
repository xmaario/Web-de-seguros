How this estimate works

The calculator starts from a sample base rate for each insurance type and adjusts it with simple multipliers for state, age range, mileage or home value, and coverage level. It then shows a range around that number to reflect how much prices can differ between providers. The sample figures are not taken from any insurer and are not actuarial data.

What affects your car insurance rate

Insurers price car insurance using many factors. The most common ones include:

  • Where you live: your state and ZIP code affect accident, theft, weather, and repair costs.
  • Your driving record: recent accidents, tickets, and claims can raise your rate for several years.
  • Age and experience: newer and younger drivers usually pay more.
  • Your car: its value, repair costs, and safety features.
  • How much you drive: higher annual mileage usually means more risk.
  • Coverage and deductibles: higher limits and added coverages cost more; higher deductibles usually cost less.
  • Credit-based insurance score: used in many states, but not all.

To see which of these you can change, read How to Lower Your Car Insurance Premium and Why Did My Car Insurance Go Up?

Real quotes consider many details this tool does not ask about, such as your driving or claims history, vehicle, home construction, ZIP code, and credit-based insurance score where permitted. Learn more in our car insurance and home insurance overviews.

Common questions

How accurate is this car insurance calculator?

It is a rough, illustrative range, not a quote. It uses simplified sample rates and only a few details, so your real price may be higher or lower. Use it to budget and to check whether quotes you receive look unusual.

Why is my quote different from the estimate?

Real quotes use your exact address, vehicle, driving and claims history, and other details this calculator does not ask for. Each insurer also weighs those factors differently, which is why comparing several quotes matters.

How much car insurance should I get before I estimate a price?

Decide on liability limits and deductibles first, so every quote uses the same coverage. Our liability limits calculator and How Much Car Insurance Do You Need? can help.

Is my information saved?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not send or store the details you enter.
