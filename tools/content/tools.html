How to use these tools

Each tool simplifies a decision that real insurers and lenders look at in much more detail. Use the result as a starting point for questions to ask, then compare actual quotes from several licensed providers with the same coverage choices. Rules, requirements and prices vary by state and provider.

If you want the background first, browse our guides or look up a term in the glossary.

Can I link to or share these tools?

Yes. Teachers, credit unions, bloggers and journalists are welcome to link to any of these pages. They are free to use, carry no sign-up, and will stay at the same address.

These tools give general information only, not insurance, legal, or financial advice, and are not offers of coverage.
