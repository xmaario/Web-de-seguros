3. Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal data for money. We share data only with service providers needed to run the website, and with advertising partners as described below:

  • Hosting and statistics provider: Cloudflare, Inc., acting as our processor for hosting and cookieless visitor statistics.
  • Email provider: Google (Gmail), which stores the messages you send us by email.
  • Contact form provider: Formspree, Inc., acting as our processor, only if the contact form is enabled.
  • Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC: for advertising through Google AdSense and, if enabled, Google Analytics. For personalized advertising, Google acts as an independent controller. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Authorities: when required by law.

4. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. In line with Google's publisher policies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the link in the footer. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

5. International transfers

Some providers, including Google LLC and Formspree, Inc., may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, request its erasure, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise your rights, email unpackinsurance@gmail.com with the subject "Data protection" and enough information for us to identify your request. We will respond within one month, which may be extended in limited cases as permitted by law.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos), www.aepd.es, or with the supervisory authority where you live.

7. Notice for US residents

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information we process, to access, correct, or delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising and of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as those terms are defined by state law.

  • Categories we process: identifiers (such as name and email if you contact us), internet or network activity (such as pages viewed and cookie identifiers used for advertising), and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Sources: you, your browser, and our advertising partners.
  • Sale and sharing: we do not sell personal information for money. The use of advertising cookies may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out as described on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.
  • Sensitive information: we do not knowingly collect it.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

To make a request, email unpackinsurance@gmail.com. You may use an authorized agent. If you disagree with our response, you may appeal by replying to it with the subject "Appeal".

8. Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and we use providers that apply appropriate technical and organizational security measures. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy
