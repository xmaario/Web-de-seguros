Do not sell or share my personal information

Last updated: November 4, 2026

UnpackInsurance does not sell personal information for money. However, the advertising cookies used to show ads on this website may be considered "selling," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" under some US state privacy laws. You can opt out at any time.

How to opt out

  1. Privacy settings: and choose to opt out. This applies to this browser and device.
  2. Global Privacy Control (GPC): where required by your state's law, we treat a GPC signal from your browser as a valid request to opt out for that browser.
  3. Email: write to contact@unpackinsurance.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share." Tell us your state of residence so we can apply the right rules.

You can also opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings and of interest-based ads from many companies at www.aboutads.info.

What happens when you opt out

You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your activity on other websites. Opting out does not affect your access to any part of UnpackInsurance.

Authorized agents

You may ask someone to submit a request for you. We may ask the agent for proof that you authorized them and may ask you to confirm the request directly.

No discrimination

We will not deny you access, charge you a different price, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
