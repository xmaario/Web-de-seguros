Cookie policy

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies this website uses and how you can control them. It is provided under Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the GDPR.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Manage your choices

You can accept, reject, or change your choice at any time. Rejecting cookies does not stop you from using the website.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Guides for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website or its partners store on your device. They can remember your preferences, help measure how a site is used, or allow advertising to be shown and measured.

Cookies we use

Our own cookies: UnpackInsurance itself does not set cookies. The estimate calculator works without storing anything on your device. We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across other websites.
