# Outreach: getting links from resource pages

Goal: 2-5 good links a month from pages that list useful consumer resources.
We offer free tools and plain-English guides. We never pay for links, never
offer link swaps, and never send the same email to everyone.

## Where to look (Google searches)

Copy one search at a time into Google (US results work best).

Library guides (best fit, often .edu or public libraries):
- `libguides "financial literacy" insurance`
- `libguides "car insurance" OR "auto insurance"`
- `library "ready reference" insurance "homeowners"`
- `inurl:libguides insurance "renters insurance"`

Teen and new drivers:
- `"teen drivers" resources "car insurance" links`
- `driving school "useful links" insurance`
- `"parents of teen drivers" resources insurance`

Car buying and loans:
- `credit union "financial education" "car buying" resources`
- `"first car" buying guide resources "gap insurance"`

Personal finance blogs:
- `"personal finance" "resources" "insurance" "tools I use"`
- `"best financial calculators" list car insurance`

## How to pick a page

Good: it already links to other outside resources, it was updated in the last
2-3 years, a real person or library maintains it, and our page fits its topic.
Skip: pages full of casino or loan spam, pages that sell links, pages with no contact.

Find the contact: look for "Contact", "Ask a librarian", the guide author's
name and email (LibGuides show it), or the site's general email.

## Rules

- 5-10 emails a week, each one personalized. Quality over volume.
- One follow-up maximum. Never argue or insist.
- Never offer money, a link back, or "guest post" swaps.
- Keep a simple log: date, site, page URL, contact, sent, follow-up, result.
- When someone adds the link, say thank you.
