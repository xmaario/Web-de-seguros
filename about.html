About UnpackInsurance

Who we are

UnpackInsurance is an independent information website that helps people in the United States understand car and home insurance. We publish plain-English explanations, guides, and an illustrative cost estimator so you can approach insurance decisions with better questions.

UnpackInsurance is operated by Mario González Ordóñez, based in Spain. Full owner details are available in our Legal Notice.

We are not an insurance company or an insurance agency. We do not sell, underwrite, or bind insurance policies, and we do not give personalized insurance advice. Only a licensed insurer or agent can provide a quote, explain the terms of a specific policy, or offer coverage.

How we are funded

Our content is free to read. UnpackInsurance is funded by advertising: we display ads served by Google AdSense, and we earn money when ads are shown or clicked. Advertisers do not choose, write, or review our articles, and an ad appearing next to our content is not an endorsement.

We do not currently receive payments from insurers, agencies, or lead-generation companies. If that changes, for example through referral or affiliate partnerships, we will update this page and clearly disclose the relationship where it appears. Such compensation could affect which providers appear on our site and where and how they appear.

Editorial standards

  • Accuracy: We aim to explain insurance concepts accurately and in plain language. Insurance rules vary by state, so we avoid presenting general information as if it applies everywhere.
  • No invented claims: We do not publish fabricated reviews, testimonials, ratings, or savings figures.
  • No price promises: Prices vary by state and provider. Our calculator shows illustrative estimates only, based on simplified sample rates.
  • Independence: Partners do not write or approve our educational content.
  • Corrections: If you spot an error, please contact us. We review reported issues and update content when needed.
  • Who is behind our content: UnpackInsurance is run by its founder and editor, Mario González Ordóñez. Articles are researched from official and publicly available sources and checked against these editorial standards before publishing. We are not licensed insurance agents, and our articles are not a substitute for advice from a licensed professional.
  • Review dates: Each guide shows the date it was last updated. We aim to review every guide at least once a year and whenever important rules change.

Important disclaimer

Everything on this site is general information. It is not legal, financial, tax, or insurance advice. Before buying a policy, read the policy documents and speak with a licensed professional about your situation.

UnpackInsurance is not an insurance company or agency. We may receive compensation from partner providers, which can affect where and how products appear. Estimates are illustrative and not offers of coverage.
